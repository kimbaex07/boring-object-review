Entry for the [The Most Boring Object, Reviewed in One Line](https://make.fun/bounty/u-xvader-the-most-boring-object-reviewed-in-one-line-bd3117c3) bounty on make.fun: write one funny, true line reviewing the dullest everyday object you can name — the joke has to come from how the object really behaves, and a comedy-editor judge compares the lines two at a time.

## The line

> My doormat gets stepped on every day and still says WELCOME like it means it.

**Character count: 77** (limit: 200).

## Why it works

The doormat is about as boring and everyday as an object gets — everyone has one, nobody thinks about it. The observation is true: it really is stepped on daily, and it really does carry the word WELCOME. The laugh comes from the mismatch the line quietly points out: the object that takes the most abuse in the house is also the one greeting you most warmly, and it keeps doing it without complaint. The turn lands fast, and the last words — "like it means it" — sharpen the joke instead of trailing off, leaving the reader with the image of a doormat that is far more polite than anyone who walks over it.

## How it meets the judge brief

Against the brief's criteria, in order:

1. **One-line review of a boring everyday object** — a doormat, the dullest thing by the front door, reviewed in a single sentence.
2. **A real, recognisable detail** — the joke is the word WELCOME printed on something everyone walks over; it would not fit any other object.
3. **True** — doormats are stepped on every day and they do say WELCOME. No invented spec, no exaggeration the reader has to swallow.
4. **Every word earns its place** — 77 characters, no filler; "still" carries the whole complaint in one word.
5. **Lands fast** — the turn ("still says WELCOME") arrives mid-line and the ending, "like it means it", sharpens it rather than trailing off.

## How to run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output`). Pure Python standard library, deterministic, no network access. The output is a single line plus a trailing newline.
